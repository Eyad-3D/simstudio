# Panel consent form (draft)

> Draft for the owner (BIZ-35). Have it checked for your country's data
> protection law (for example the EU's GDPR) before use.

**LightSim user panel: what you agree to**

- You will take part in video sessions of about 45 minutes, about once
  per LightSim release, for as long as you wish. You can leave at any
  time without giving a reason.
- In a session you try tasks in LightSim and say what you think. We note
  how long tasks take, where you get stuck and what you say.
- **Recording** (tick one): [ ] the session may be recorded (screen and
  voice), and the recording is deleted within 90 days; [ ] no recording,
  notes only.
- **Your data.** You choose what to show. Nothing you show, such as your
  car's values, is copied or kept without your written permission.
- **How we keep notes.** Notes use a seat code (such as FS-2), not your
  name or your team's or company's name. Your contact details are kept
  separately, only to arrange sessions, and deleted when you leave the
  panel.
- **Quotes.** We may quote what you said, without your name, in
  LightSim's planning documents. [ ] You may also name me / my team in
  the release notes' thanks.
- **Licence.** Taking part does not change the licence you use LightSim
  under. [Industry seats: your panel use is covered by the evaluation
  permission attached.]
- **Contact** for questions or to have your data deleted: [address].

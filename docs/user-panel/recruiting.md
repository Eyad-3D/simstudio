## Invitation: Formula Student team

Subject: Try LightSim on your car's concept — a small user panel

Hello [team name],

I make LightSim, a desktop app for vehicle energy and powertrain
simulation that is free for Formula Student teams. It answers questions
such as your 75 m acceleration time under the 80 kW limit and your
endurance energy, from parts on a diagram.

I am looking for five electric FS teams to try each new release on their
own car and tell me what works and what does not. It takes one 45-minute
video call per release (about every [two] months), with one or two team
members. You use your own data; nothing you show leaves the call without
your permission.

In return: [thank-you, owner's decision], and your problems go straight
to the people who decide what is built next.

Interested? Reply to [address] with the name of the person to talk to.

[name]

## Screening questions

Ask before the first session; keep the answers with the seat code only.

1. Seat type: FS team / industry engineer / IT or CAE gatekeeper.
2. Your role, in a few words (for example "powertrain lead", "energy
   management engineer", "CAE tools owner").
3. Which simulation tools do you use today, and for what?
4. Which question did you last answer with simulation? How long did it
   take?
5. Your computer: Windows or Linux, and can you install software
   yourself, or does IT do it?
6. FS only: electric or combustion? Which competitions this season?
7. Can you join a 45-minute video call about every [two] months for at
   least three releases?

## How it computes

Its output is its *Value*, at every moment of the run: out = *Value*. It has
no unit of its own: the number reaches the input it is wired to as a number
in that input's unit as the app shows it (a *Value* of 50 wired to a speed
input is 50 km/h). A case or a sweep can set a different *Value* for its
runs.

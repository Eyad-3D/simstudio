# See what limits the car

A band along the bottom of the *Results* chart says, at every moment, what
held the car back: the tyres' grip, the motor or engine, the battery, a
power limit you set, or nothing. Under the chart, a legend gives the time
spent in each.

## Read the band

1. Run a case and look at the *Results* chart, with time on the x axis.
2. Read the colours along its bottom edge. Each driveline (each axle a
   motor drives) has its own strip.
3. Read the legend under the chart: each state's colour, name and time.
   Point at a state's name to see what it means.
4. Click **What limits the car** to hide or show the band.

On the Formula Student car's *Acceleration 75 m*, the rear tyres slip for
the first 1.92 s, then the 80 kW power limit holds the car for 2.05 s, which
adds up to the run's 3.97 s.

## The states

Each solver step is named by the first of these that applies:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Braking | The driver braked, with the friction brakes or by regeneration |
| Tyre grip | A driven wheel was at its tyres' grip limit: it slipped |
| Set power limit | The battery's *Output Power Limit* held the motors back |
| Battery or supply | The battery, fuel cell or DC-DC converter could not give more (hatched) |
| Motor or engine | The driver asked for full torque, or a motor was at its maximum speed |
| Coasting | No drive and no braking were asked for |
| Driver's demand met | Nothing held the car back |

The band is drawn on a time axis only. With the distance on the x axis,
the legend still gives the times.

# Can I use LightSim for …? (draft)

> **Draft for the owner (BIZ-29).** This page explains LightSim's licence in
> plain words. It is not the licence itself and not legal advice: the
> end-user licence agreement (`EULA.txt`, the terms you accept when you
> install) decides. The *Today* column reads the EULA that ships now
> (version 1.0). The *EULA 1.1* column reads the [proposed
> version](EULA-proposal.md), which is **not in force** until the owner
> approves it and a release ships it.

LightSim is proprietary software (the owner keeps all rights) that is
**free for non-commercial use**. Non-commercial means learning, research
and hobby work without a business purpose. Using it at or for a company
needs a commercial licence.

- **Yes**: you may, free of charge.
- **No**: you need a commercial licence.
- **Ask**: the EULA does not answer it clearly; ask the owner before you
  rely on it (see [How to ask](#how-to-ask-about-a-licence)).

## How to ask about a licence

- **Today (EULA 1.0):** the EULA says to open an issue at
  <https://github.com/Eyad-3D/simstudio>. Issues are public: write only
  what you are happy for anyone to read.
- **Planned (EULA 1.1):** a private address, **[private address]** (the
  owner's decision D1 in the [proposal](EULA-proposal.md#owner-decisions)).
  Licence and purchase questions will never need a public post.

## Words used on this page

- **Commercial licence:** a paid, written licence from the owner for use
  at or for a business.
- **Evaluation:** trying LightSim to decide whether it fits your work, not
  to produce real work.
- **Unchanged installer:** the installer file exactly as downloaded from
  LightSim's release page; its checksum (a fingerprint of the file)
  proves it is unchanged.
- **CI (continuous integration):** jobs that run software automatically,
  for example on every change to a repository.

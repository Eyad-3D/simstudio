# LightSim Contributor Licence Agreement (draft)

> **Draft for the owner (BIZ-13). Not in use yet.** No outside code can be
> accepted until the owner and a lawyer have approved a final version.
> This is **not legal advice**. The grant follows the structure of widely
> used individual CLAs (such as the Apache Software Foundation's), with
> one change LightSim needs: the owner may license contributions under
> any terms, including commercial and proprietary ones.

## In plain words

- You keep the copyright in what you contribute.
- You give the owner a permanent, free licence to use, change and
  license your contribution to others, under any terms, including in the
  paid version of LightSim.
- You also license any of your patents that your contribution needs.
- You confirm the work is yours to give, and that your employer agrees if
  it might own it.
- The owner does not have to use your contribution.

## Owner decisions

- Whether to accept outside code at all, or to keep code closed and take
  only issues, ideas and models (the simplest choice; JetBrains works this
  way).
- A corporate version for contributors whose employer owns their work.
- How to sign: a signed PDF by e-mail, or a CLA service (such as CLA
  Assistant) that records the signature on the pull request. Either way,
  add the account to `.github/CLA-SIGNATORIES.txt` with the date.
- A separate, shorter translator agreement for translations.
